How the ₦2 Billion Goal is Deployed

Establishing and sustaining operations over 5 years requires strategic capital distribution to ensure community ownership, staff food security, skills training, and stable income.

Samiya Hub ₦1.4 BILLION (70%)

Facility construction, equipment, and 5-year hub operation.

GOSA Community Projects Fund ₦400 MILLION (20%)

Direct community enablement and micro-initiatives.

Alumnifest Future ₦200 MILLION (10%)

Long-term endowment fund for sustainability.
